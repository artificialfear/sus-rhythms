# Sus Rhythms · Raven Band Edition

A clapping social-deduction game for band class. Crewmates clap the rhythm perfectly, and the Imposters secretly mess it up. Call Emergency Meetings, vote, and send suspects out the airlock.

- **Teacher (ViewBoard):** https://artificialfear.github.io/sus-rhythms/?teacher
- **Students (iPads):** https://artificialfear.github.io/sus-rhythms/, then tap **Check my secret role** (or scan the QR code on the board)

Everything runs in the browser, with no accounts or server. Class rosters stay on the device they're typed into.

Fan-made classroom game. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the makers of any video game.

Music notation: [VexFlow](https://www.vexflow.com/) (MIT). QR codes: qrcode-generator by Kazuhiko Arase (MIT).
